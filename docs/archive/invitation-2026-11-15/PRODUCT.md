# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Persian-speaking customers and industry visitors invited by Zamanian Trading to Faraz 3.

## Product Purpose
A single digital invitation: recognize Persepolis and its representative, find exhibition logistics and booth ۲۳۲, then open directions or call.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static Next.js page, mobile first, centered RTL composition, no forms or backend. At default text size fit 320×568, 360×640 and 390×844 without scrolling. Allow natural scrolling with enlarged text. Preserve all supplied event facts and contacts; venue is a name-based map search, not a verified pin.

## Brand Commitments
Original complete red Persepolis logo, warm white and charcoal, no Faraz artwork or palette. Formal, warm and concise Persian. User approved light ceramic material with a red edge and direct implementation in code.

## Evidence on Hand
Original asset archive, original logo and references, config/event.ts, and official organizer research recorded in docs/content.md. Generated material is conceptual, not a verified product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic RTL HTML, isolated telephone numbers, keyboard focus, readable contrast, touch targets at least 44px and natural text reflow.
