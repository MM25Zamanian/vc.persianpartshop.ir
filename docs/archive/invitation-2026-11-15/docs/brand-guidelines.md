# Brand guidelines

The complete original red Persepolis logo is used unchanged from the supplied transparent PNG. Preserve its aspect ratio, colors and clear space. No AI-generated, redrawn, cropped, filtered or reconstructed mark. Its underlying supplied JPEG limits fine print quality; no official vector has been supplied.

The approved implementation plan uses suggested package tokens: red #ED0812, deep red #AE0F17, charcoal #292727, warm white #FAF8F6, ivory #F5F0EC and line #DED5D0. These are not certified print colors. The logo and booth are the primary red anchors; the main action is charcoal so it does not compete with the booth.

Faraz imagery is an information reference only. Do not use its navy palette, concrete numeral or calligraphic identity. No gold or additional accent hue. Brand hierarchy is Persepolis, Zamanian Trading and its representative relationship, then exhibition and logistics.

A generated light ceramic study at the header edges supplies restrained material depth. It is conceptual and not a photograph of actual products. Original logo and all meaningful text remain separate HTML/image assets in the clean central area. Vazirmatn Variable is locally hosted under SIL OFL 1.1. No artificial Persian tracking.
