# Visual acceptance criteria

- Compare actual screenshots at 320, 375, 390, 430, 768 and 1440px and a short viewport against the approved reference plus later design-system corrections. Record screenshots, viewport and differences.
- The centers of the primary content containers and actions align with the main content axis within 2 CSS pixels, except where a documented element's visual bounds intentionally differ; inspect the perceptual balance as well.
- Original complete logo loads without distortion, recoloring, cropping or added ornament; compare to the supplied asset.
- All supplied event name, dates, hours, venue, prominent booth ۲۳۲ and next actions remain readable and accurate; no fabricated facts or product imagery.
- At tested viewports and 200% zoom, no text or controls overlap, clip, or cause unintended horizontal scrolling; normal vertical scrolling is acceptable. Persian joining and mixed-direction numbers render correctly.
- Primary actions are at least 44px high, have accessible names, work with keyboard, show focus, and link to their stated destination. Check contrast against applicable WCAG thresholds and reduced motion where motion exists.
- Review final social image at preview scale and in actual target consumers where reachable; record cache, crawler and unfurl limitations. Critical defects block completion. Neither screenshots nor self-reported skill use alone prove design quality.
