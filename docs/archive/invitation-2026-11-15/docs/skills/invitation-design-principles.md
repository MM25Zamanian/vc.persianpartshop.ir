# Invitation design principles

A corporate invitation should convey recognition and respectful hospitality, then answer what, when, where and how to act. Separate attraction, logistics and next action; keep the host and event relationship accurate. A recipient may return to a shared link for details, so do not sacrifice date, hours, venue or booth to a large hero. Place booth ۲۳۲ prominently and label map and call destinations clearly.

Use a calm centered hierarchy, generous but purposeful negative space, consistent visual rhythm and restrained red, warm white and charcoal. Balance content geometrically, not just text alignment. Use the complete original mark and protect clear space. Persian type must shape naturally, wrap comfortably and remain readable at actual mobile scale. Avoid decorative imagery on top of text. The current design system calls for no decorative backgrounds; conceptual architectural material may be considered only if that direction changes. Never describe generated imagery as a verified product.

Digital distribution requires reflow, accessible controls, accurate preview metadata and testing the delivered link preview; a poster-sized mockup alone is not evidence. Distinguish visual hierarchy from physical reading order in RTL. Do not invent logistics or claim an unverified URL or map pin.
