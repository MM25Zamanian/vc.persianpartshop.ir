# Persian typography

Use a licensed typeface with full Persian joining, glyphs, numerals and marks; the current project uses local PeydaWeb WOFF2 fixed weights400/600/700/800 via next/font/local. Render actual text to assess shaping and baseline. Use clear weight contrast without synthetic distortion; generous enough line height prevents clipped ascenders and descenders. Never apply artificial letter spacing to Persian as a luxury effect. Preserve natural word boundaries and avoid awkward isolated words; allow wrapping and zoom. Use Persian digits for business copy as supplied; isolate LTR telephone numbers and Latin fragments with semantic bidi isolation, and test mixed punctuation and numerals in RTL. At 320–375px and enlarged text confirm readability and no clipping, overlap or horizontal scrolling.
